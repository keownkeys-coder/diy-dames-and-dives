Fav track I can do what I want but man animaru is such a good track, they can really do heavy oh my goshhhh

Really great groove, interesting how the acoustic guitar can almost sound distorted when she's smacking it. Love the use of Dynamics and obviously the switching languages is dope

It's really interesting how all of the disparate jazzy accompaniment that's usually building groove and space and building out the harmony, but when they all land on single hits they can really smack them WOW

The dynamics are something I really want to be able to get a hold of in my music AND man that roomy drum sound especially track 4 but it feels so good to turn up while also still sounding full and dynamic and not headbanging volume

The bassist is really the deciding factor on whether or not smth is heavy huh, if they're doing unison UGH beautiful but if they're walking we're grooving flowers to the bassist for walking that line really well

Heaviest song so far (or just about) into a very subdued jazzy duet (with some vocal overdubs shhh🤫) good sequencing, mad respect for going for a bass solo on an upright, still pretty groovy tho,

On Norwegian Shag they do this great push pull where it feels like a lo where you're waiting for the groove to come back they play with anticipation so we'll especially on the chorus-y sounding bit I think that's like a jpop progression in the prechorus? Or the royal road maybe IV-V-vi-I? I dunno

Man I love electric guitars lol, theyre sparsely added on the album but every time they show up MMMM it's juicy I love it, and loud quiet y know I just dearly love it

Rat with wings the acoustic guitar solo kind of part, while there's a lot of shine on the ac gtr this album I feel the runs in the call and response es que section reallllly land the first one more so than the second but that first one mmm feels magical the last one too is great too

Oo speedy I love quick tempos, I love the stereo (technically binaural in this case) image I'm not sure if they just recorded live in a room or what that process looked like, but everything has its own spot and the drums are also really spread out in a way that I like I don't even know if kick and snare are down the middle but jazz you can do things like that. Honestly kind of cute when they do the duduNAH kinda beat I feel like they use it a good amount haha

The be quiet on the be quiet :0

Why is having the last song in 3 such a mood, like I think the last song (XO)? on from under the cork tree does it too and it just does rules. What time signature is the last American highway flower

Very orchestral on the last song too very beautiful, the string section getting to sing more (they've been ripping the whole time tho) but they're very prominent here, love the flamenco romantic strumming too, build stop build also just rules

I wish I knew Japanese. Not for the anime or the games but the muuuusic UGH 😭 such a great build thru out the last song. Good sequencing feels very Last Song

And last song to first feels nice, like oo jazzy solo guitar works well as an intro and a transition

Overall great album, it'll be interesting if they continue writing songs in this specific style they've made (jazzy and Midwest emo style accompaniment alongside very indie jazz vocals and some chunks of rock in there sometimes) or if they'll be branching out in certain directions (it's my heavy need for heavy indeed but I'd love to hear a no acoustic gtr album see what that sounds like but also the ac gtr is so fun grrrr) on later releases either way I'm excited!
